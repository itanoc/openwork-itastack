# How to Factory Reset your IP Phone

> Source: https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/factory-reset-guides/

The guides below will take you through the process needed to bring back the factory defaults settings of your IP Phone. This step must be done before provisioning your IP Phone with 3CX in case the device has residual settings of a previous configuration.

## Preferred

- [Fanvil IP and Infrastructure phones](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/factory-reset-fanvil-x-series/)
- [Yealink T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, CP and VP Series](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/factory-reset-yealink/)
- [Snom IP Phones and Conference phones](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/factory-reset-snom/)

## Supported

- [Gigaset DECT Series](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/gigaset-n670-n870/#h.7g8xv7ys5ba3): N670, N870
- [Grandstream GRP and GXP Series](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/factory-reset-grandstream-gxp/)
- [Grandstream PA speaker - GSC35 Series: 3505, 3510](https://www.3cx.com/sip-phones/grandstream-speaker-intercom/#h.2cs7nylzskcd)
